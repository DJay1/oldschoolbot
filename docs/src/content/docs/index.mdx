NEED TO REPLACE is a Discord bot based around Old School RuneScape. It has commands for account stats and for simulating loot from monsters and clues.

## Minions

Minions let you simulate playing a virtual RuneScape account in Discord. You control a minion, who you send out to do various tasks, like killing monsters for loot, completing clue scrolls, and training skills. With the loot they get, you can craft items, sell them, and trade to other real players.

You can also read the [FAQ](./getting-started/faq/).

Minions are entirely virtual, and not in any way tradeable for real GP or real money. It's simulating the real game for fun. Do not break any of the official OSRS rules. Read the rules here: [NEED TO REPLACE rules](./getting-started/rules/).

## Getting Started

**To get started, firstly read the** [**rules**](./getting-started/rules/), then get yourself a minion by typing [[/minion buy]]. To get your starting gp, you can use the [[/minion daily]] command.

Next, decide what you want to do. Skilling? PVM? Questing? Have a look around the rest of the wiki to see what you can do, and how - and just do whatever you want to do!

For a guide on items you can buy from the bot and how shop pricing works, check out [Buyable Items and Shop Pricing Mechanics](./osb/buyables/buyable-items-and-shop-pricing-mechanics).
